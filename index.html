What we offer

Two simple ways to store

Pick the option that fits what you're storing. Both are rented by the month at our secured Lahowal compound, and you can upgrade or move out whenever you like.

Floor space

A marked, measured patch of floor that's yours for the month. Best for bulky, awkward things you'd rather stack yourself — trunks, mattresses, furniture, cartons, a scooter.

  • Sizes from 2×2 ft up to 8×8 ft
  • Stack as high as you sensibly can
  • Ground floor, easy loading access

Lockable shelf units

An enclosed unit with a door you padlock yourself. Nobody else has a key — including us. Right for documents, electronics, tools, stock and anything you'd rather keep out of sight.

  • Small (2×2×2 ft) and medium (3×3×2 ft)
  • Bring your own lock, or buy one here
  • Dust-protected and off the ground
