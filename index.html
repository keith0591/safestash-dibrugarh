What we offer

Two simple ways to store

Pick whichever suits what you're storing. Both are rented by the month inside our store room at Lahoal, and you can change size or move out whenever you like.

Floor space

A marked, measured patch of floor inside the store room that's yours for the month. Best for bulky, awkward things you'd rather stack yourself — trunks, mattresses, furniture, cartons, or a two-wheeler.

  • Sizes from 2×2 ft up to 8×8 ft
  • Stack as high as you sensibly can
  • Dry floor, under a solid roof
  • Two-wheelers parked inside, not out in the open

Lockable shelf units

A shelf unit raised off the ground with a padlock on it — we supply the lock, so there's nothing for you to arrange. Right for documents, electronics, tools, stock and anything you'd rather keep off the floor and out of sight.

  • Small (2×2×2 ft) and medium (3×3×2 ft)
  • Padlock provided by us
  • Raised off the ground and dust-protected

How it works

No paperwork marathon, no lock-in

We've kept this deliberately simple. Message us, bring your things, pay for the months you use.

  1. Message us on WhatsApp

    Tell us roughly what you're storing and for how long. We'll suggest a size and confirm the price — usually within the hour.

  2. Bring your things over

    You arrange your own transport. We're just off the highway behind the Indian Oil petrol pump, so a vehicle can pull right up to the door.

  3. We lock it up

    Floor space gets marked out for you; shelf units get a padlock, which we provide. Everything sits inside the store room under a solid roof.

  4. Leave whenever you want

    There's no notice period and no minimum term. You pay only for the months you occupy the space — if you want to clear out tomorrow, that's fine.

Not sure if we can store it? Some things we can take, some we can't — just ask us on WhatsApp and we'll tell you straight away. Same for anything unusually large or heavy.
